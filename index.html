Booking Confirmation, Payment & Cancellation Policy
Please review the following information before confirming your booking with Ivory Arc Travels.
To confirm your selected itinerary, Ivory Arc Travels requires a booking commitment payment of INR 5,000 (or the equivalent in your selected currency). This payment allows us to:
  • Lock hotel rates with suppliers.
  • Reserve transport and operational resources.
  • Allocate a dedicated Journey Manager.
  • Begin pre-tour planning activities.
  • Ensure resources are reserved for genuine travel enquiries.
The INR 5,000 commitment payment is not an additional fee and will be adjusted against the total tour cost.
You may revise the itinerary after making the INR 5,000 booking commitment payment — generate a new estimate, add or remove services, modify hotels, sightseeing or travel dates. No additional commitment payment is required; the original INR 5,000 is automatically adjusted against the revised booking.

IMPORTANT: Once Ivory Arc Travels issues the Final GST-Compliant Invoice, no changes shall be allowed — including dates, hotels, vehicle, sightseeing, route, guest count, package modifications or any itinerary revisions. The Final GST-Compliant Invoice is the final booking confirmation document for operational, accounting and taxation purposes.
Booking Commitment Payment — INR 5,000, due immediately upon booking confirmation.

Second Payment — 50% of total tour value, due at least 7 days before the tour start date. The INR 5,000 commitment forms part of this 50% and is adjusted automatically.

Example: Total INR 100,000 → 50% = INR 50,000 → already paid INR 5,000 → balance due INR 45,000.

Final Payment — remaining balance, due 24 hours before the tour start date. On receipt of the 50% payment we generate the Final GST-Compliant Invoice, send it to you, and lock the itinerary from further modifications.
More than 7 days before start: 100% refund of the INR 5,000 commitment. No cancellation fee.

7 days to 48 hours before: 100% refund of all amounts paid, excluding GST already invoiced and deposited.

48 to 24 hours before: 85% refund of all amounts paid (excluding GST); 15% retained for supplier commitments and operational expenses.

Within 24 hours of start: no refund of commitment or advance payment — hotels, transport and resources are already confirmed.

Within 24 hours after full payment: 50% of total tour value refunded; GST non-refundable.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment source.
  • Refund processing timeline is 7–10 business days.
  • GST already invoiced and deposited is non-refundable.
  • Acceptance of this policy forms part of the booking contract between you and Ivory Arc Travels.
  • Ivory Arc Travels reserves the right to review exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis.
You must accept the Booking Confirmation, Payment & Cancellation Policy before proceeding to payment.
